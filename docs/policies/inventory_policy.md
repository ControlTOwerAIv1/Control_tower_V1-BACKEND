# Inventory Policy

## Reorder Policy
- Reorder is recommended when current stock is at or below reorder point.
- Reorder point is driven by average daily sales and lead time.
- Buffer days should be applied to reduce stockout risk.

## Stockout Priority
- Items with lower days left are prioritized first.
- Products with active sales and low stock should be expedited.

## Dead Inventory
- Products not sold for 60 or more days are treated as dead or slow-moving.
- Items never sold should be reviewed for liquidation or promotion.
